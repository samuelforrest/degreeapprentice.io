Join a Lloyds Banking Group technology team in Manchester and earn a degree from Manchester Metropolitan University while you work. You'll learn to code, solve problems and build digital products that millions of customers use.

## What to expect

- Four years, starting in **September 2027**
- In the office most days while you settle in, then hybrid working (at least two days a week in the office)
- Your salary is re-graded into the normal pay range once you finish

## How to apply

Apply online, then complete the online assessments. If you're successful, you'll be invited to an assessment centre in **February 2027**.

You'll need the right to work in the UK for the whole programme. Lloyds can't offer visa sponsorship for this role.
