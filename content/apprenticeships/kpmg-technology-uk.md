A four-year apprenticeship in KPMG's technology teams that ends with a BSc (Hons) in Digital and Technology Solutions, delivered by BPP.

You apply to a specific KPMG office. Your work is spread across KPMG offices, client sites and some home working. Tuition is a mix of face-to-face sessions in Birmingham or Manchester and live online classes.

## Before you apply

KPMG hasn't published 2027 dates yet. You can sign up on its careers site to be told when applications open. If you're a few grades short of the entry requirements, KPMG still encourages you to apply.
