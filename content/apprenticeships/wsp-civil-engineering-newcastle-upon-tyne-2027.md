A Level 6 civil engineer apprenticeship with WSP, studying with University of Northumbria at Newcastle.

- **Start date:** 6 September 2027
- **Duration:** 5 years 8 months
- **Hours:** 37 hours 30 minutes a week

- GCSE: 5 GCSEs including English, Maths, Science (Level 5 or above)
- A Level: 3 A Levels to include Phys Science & B in Maths (BBC or above)
