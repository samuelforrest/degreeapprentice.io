A Level 6 civil engineer apprenticeship with WSP, studying with University of Exeter.

## Key facts

- **Start date:** 6 September 2027
- **Duration:** 5 years 8 months
- **Hours:** 37 hours 30 minutes a week
- **Positions:** 1
- **Training course:** Civil engineer (Level 6)

## Entry requirements

- GCSE: 5 GCSEs including English, Maths, Science (Level 5 or above)
- A Level: 3 A Levels to Include Maths and Science (ABB)
- BTEC: L3 Extended in an Engineering-related subject (DDM (must include Maths))
