BAE Systems pays your tuition fees and a salary while you earn a full honours degree. Its degree apprenticeships cover **Aerospace Engineering, Nuclear Engineering, Software Engineering, Information & Technology, Enterprise Architecture, Project Management and Management**. The aerospace engineering scheme lasts five years.

## When to apply

Applications for 2027 open in **January 2027**, and BAE advises applying early. The main recruitment window runs for about six weeks.

## Security and eligibility

Every applicant needs at least Baseline Personnel Security Standard clearance. Many roles need National Security Vetting, which usually requires 5 to 10 years of continuous UK residency. Because of export controls, your nationality or place of birth may limit which roles you can do.

In Scotland the requirement is 5 National 5s at grade C or above (including English, Maths and a Science) plus 3 to 4 Highers.
