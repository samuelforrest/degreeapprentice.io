Degree apprenticeships don't share one deadline the way UCAS does. Each employer opens and closes its own applications, and many close early or as soon as they have enough candidates. This timeline is for starting in the September after you finish Year 13. Dates vary, so treat it as a guide and check each listing.

## Year 12: spring and summer

- **Work out which careers interest you.** Look at what roles exist in a sector, not just which companies you've heard of.
- **Get some experience.** Work experience, insight days, virtual internships, part-time jobs and clubs all give you examples for applications and interviews.
- **Start a shortlist.** Browse [current roles](/roles) to see which employers recruit apprentices, what they ask for and when they opened last year.
- **Draft a CV**, even a simple one. Many applications ask for one.

## September to December: the busiest time

Many large employers, especially in finance, consulting, technology and engineering, open applications in the autumn of Year 13.

- **Apply early.** Some schemes review applications as they arrive and close when they're full, sometimes before the advertised date.
- **Expect online assessments** soon after applying. [Practise the common formats](/tests) before your first one.
- **Keep a list** of where you've applied, deadlines, logins and what stage you're at. A simple spreadsheet works.
- **Apply to university as well if you're unsure.** The UCAS deadline for most courses is at the end of January. See [applying to both](/guides/applying-to-uni-and-apprenticeships).

## January to April: interviews and assessment centres

- **Prepare for video interviews and assessment centres.** See our guides to [video interviews](/guides/video-interviews) and [assessment centres](/guides/assessment-centres).
- **Keep applying.** Many smaller employers, and some large ones, open later in the year.
- **Offers start arriving.** They usually depend on your exam results.

## May to August: exams, results and starting

- **Some roles are still advertised** through the summer, especially at smaller employers. It's worth checking regularly.
- **Focus on your exams.** Most offers are conditional on your grades.
- **After results day,** confirm your place with your employer and sort out any paperwork, such as right-to-work checks.

## If you miss the autumn rush

Don't panic. Roles are advertised all year, and many start in January or later as well as September. Check [open roles](/roles), which are sorted by closing date, and filter by sector to see what's open now.
